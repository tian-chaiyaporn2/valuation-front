Free-float-adjusted market-cap weights across developed and emerging large, mid and small caps. Observations must come from the same index family; methodology breaks require a new series identifier.

  • Evidence is classified as canonical, investable proxy, approved proxy, qualitative, or context only. Approved substitutes receive at most 50% component coverage; investable ETF return proxies and point-in-time qualitative structural inputs are labeled separately.
  • Approved earnings or benchmark proxies can inform descriptive claim states, but cannot support regional Confirmed/Secular Reversal or a GLOBAL_REVERSAL/SECULAR_REGIME_CHANGE until canonical evidence replaces them.
  • Holdings are not flows; holdings changes are never relabeled as subscriptions or transactions.
  • Fund domicile and custodian location are not treated as investor nationality.
  • Benchmark-weight changes can occur without trading and are kept separate from capital-flow evidence.
  • ETF adjusted-close series are investable total-return proxies in USD, not canonical regional indices or the canonical benchmark-weight series.
  • Qualitative assessments are point-in-time editable inputs and must not be applied retrospectively in a backtest.
