Methodology & provenance

One benchmark, explicit gaps

Canonical benchmarkMSCI ACWI IMI
ProviderMSCI
CurrencyUSD
Update date2026-08-31

Free-float-adjusted market-cap weights across developed and emerging large, mid and small caps. Observations must come from the same index family; methodology breaks require a new series identifier.

  • Evidence is classified as canonical, approved proxy, or context only. Approved proxies receive at most 50% component coverage and cap confirmation at PROVISIONAL.
  • Approved earnings or benchmark proxies cannot support Confirmed Reversal, Secular Reversal, or a normal global regime until canonical evidence replaces them.
  • Holdings are not flows; holdings changes are never relabeled as subscriptions or transactions.
  • Fund domicile and custodian location are not treated as investor nationality.
  • Benchmark-weight changes can occur without trading and are kept separate from capital-flow evidence.
  • ETF adjusted-close series are investable total-return proxies in USD, not the canonical benchmark-weight series.
  • Qualitative assessments are point-in-time editable inputs and must not be applied retrospectively in a backtest.

Research monitor only. Approved proxies are visibly labeled, receive at most half component coverage, and can never produce normal, Confirmed, or Secular reversal status. Licensed point-in-time index earnings and canonical benchmark history remain explicit dependencies.
